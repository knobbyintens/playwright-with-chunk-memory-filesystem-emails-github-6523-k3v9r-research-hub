summary: "A compact subword tokenizer pipeline that reaches competitive fertility on low-resource languages with a fraction of the vocabulary."
tags: "tokenization, low-resource, multilingual"
---

Large vocabularies inflate embedding tables without helping languages with little written data. This project studies a compact subword pipeline that keeps fertility competitive on low-resource languages while shrinking the vocabulary several-fold.
